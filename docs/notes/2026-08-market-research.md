## Working Conclusion

The immediate opportunity is a chef-specific resume tool, not another broad restaurant job board.
The tool should convert an employment-history skeleton into a culinary-career record that shows actual roles, stations, responsibilities, skills, equipment, and representative experience.
Generic PDF extraction is only an input shortcut.

The long-term opportunity remains a professional hiring network where culinary professionals and verified restaurants evaluate concrete fit before they meet.
The operator reports that compensation and working conditions often remain undisclosed until the final interview stage, while some well-known restaurants attract applicants despite reported compensation below the statutory minimum.
This repository has not independently measured the prevalence of that practice.

## Employment-Import Baseline

### Work24

The Ministry of Employment and Labor [announced the Work24 integrated career certificate](https://www.moel.go.kr/news/enews/report/enewsView.do?news_seq=19692) on 2026-07-29.
The announcement states that the initial service combines 24 types of career data and can use selected records to populate a Work24 resume.
It also states a plan to provide career data to private employment platforms with the job seeker's consent from November 2026.
As of this review date, that private-platform capability is a future plan rather than a current integration available to this project.
The eligible operators, access procedure, interface, and cost are `[UNKNOWN]` from the announcement.

The official [Work24 service introduction](https://www.work24.go.kr/cm/d/a/0112/retrieveCarrCrtfItcn.do) states that occupation information comes from the initial employment-insurance acquisition report.
It also states that later personnel transfers or role changes are not separately updated.
This limitation supports treating public employment data as a starting skeleton rather than a complete culinary-career record.

### Saramin

Saramin's current [privacy policy](https://www.saramin.co.kr/zf_user/help/privacy) describes a career-import flow that connects to the National Health Insurance Service through identity-verification providers.
It states that identity information used for authenticity checks is destroyed immediately after the check and is not stored separately.
Saramin's official [career-certification page](https://m.saramin.co.kr/promotion/career-cert?event_idx=534%3Finner_source%3Dsaramin&inner_campaign=m_menu&inner_medium=banner&inner_term=ca-promo) presents automatic entry of prior employers and tenure together with a career-certification badge.

These features show that employer-and-period import is not a sufficient product differentiator.
The proposed differentiation is an inference: help a culinary professional reconstruct what they actually owned, practiced, and achieved in each kitchen.

## Privacy and Consent Baseline

Article 16 of the Personal Information Protection Act requires a controller to collect only the [minimum personal information necessary for the stated purpose](https://www.law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1029335669).
Articles 15 and 17 identify notice requirements for consent to collection, use, and [third-party provision](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1006183947).
The Personal Information Protection Commission explains that [resident registration number processing is restricted](https://m.pipc.go.kr/np/cop/bbs/selectBoardArticle.do?bbsId=BS074&mCode=C020010000&nttId=11381) to specific legal grounds.

These sources support a browser-local document boundary, separate talent-pool consent, and omission of identity fields that the resume import does not need.
They do not by themselves complete a legal review for the eventual product.

## Direct Competitor Observation

[Foodscout](https://foodnscout.com/) is the closest domestic hiring competitor identified in the supplied research.
Its public home page currently presents paid placement tiers, general job listings, and resume listings.
The same page states that its professional recruitment section has no information.

Foodscout's [service guide](https://foodnscout.com/work/services.html) describes banner placement, highlighted listings, automatic listing bumps, and paid access to resume contact details.
Its public business model therefore appears to emphasize listing distribution and visibility.

A current [sample job listing](https://foodnscout.com/work/employ_detail.html?no=864) uses conventional fields for role, location, duties, employment type, compensation, benefits, qualifications, and application method.
The sample lists compensation as negotiable and the company headcount as missing.

These public observations support the differentiation hypothesis.
They do not establish what Foodscout offers after login, through manual operations, or through unpublished services.

## Product Implications

The resume tool should:

- Preserve the original employer name while allowing a separate restaurant display name.
- Keep qualification dates separate from person-confirmed employment dates.
- Use structured culinary questions before free-form writing.
- Distinguish imported, person-confirmed, and person-authored information.
- Produce one editable resume layout.
- Ask for talent-pool participation only after resume completion and default to private.

The first resume slice should not include OCR, direct government-service integration, multiple templates, AI embellishment, public profiles, job recommendations, or a mobile application.

The hiring network remains focused on full-time jobs that pass minimum verification.
Restaurant and culinary professional profiles must expose concrete facts that affect retention.
Required compensation and working-condition fields cannot use placeholders such as "negotiable," "company policy," or "to be discussed later."
An employer that does not pass minimum verification is not listed and does not receive introductions.
